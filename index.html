Inspiring Excellence, Leadership, and Public Service

The KFUEIT CSS Club is a student-driven platform dedicated to empowering students who aspire to join Pakistan’s civil services. Established in 2020, the club provides guidance, learning resources, expert sessions, and a collaborative environment to help students prepare for the Central Superior Services (CSS) examination and develop leadership skills.

CSS

Aim is to provide Information,Material and road map to students which will lead them to achieve their goal of clearing CSS.

ONE TEST

Aim is to prepare students for major test PPSC,FPSC,NTS by giving guidelines mock tests and quizzes.

Peer Learning Community

Study groups, debate competitions, and collaborative learning.
